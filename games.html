HOW TO PLAY

  1. Choose a world to explore.
  2. Pick any color you like from the palette.
  3. Tap an object to color it in.
  4. Watch it wake up and come alive!
  5. Color every object in the scene.
  6. Earn stars and unlock the next world.

ABOUT COLORQUEST

ColorQuest is a little coloring world where nothing sits still for long. Pick any color you like, touch an object, and it wakes up — wings start flapping, petals open, rockets lift off. Play through thirteen worlds — Forest, Garden, Ocean, Space, Dinosaur, Home, School, Road, Hotel, Cafe, Restaurant, Office and Room — one unlocked at a time. Your progress is remembered for this session — refreshing the page starts a fresh journey.
